How V6.4 works

  1. Move the map to the river stretch you want.
  2. Tap Refresh Heat Map.
  3. The overlay stays fixed until you refresh again.
  4. Green areas are the best theoretical test zones.
  5. Tap a colored area to analyze that exact spot.

This version intentionally disables auto-refresh so the results do not jump around while you pan or zoom.
